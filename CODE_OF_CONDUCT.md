# Community conduct

SimVLA welcomes questions, bug reports, and contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. In project spaces, be respectful, describe technical disagreements with evidence, and give others room to ask basic questions. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may limit participation when needed. For ordinary conduct questions, contact the maintainers through the [project repository](https://github.com/kyounginbaik/SimVLA). Do not put confidential conduct details in a public issue. A maintainer-approved private conduct reporting address has not yet been published; confidential reporting remains a release follow-up.
