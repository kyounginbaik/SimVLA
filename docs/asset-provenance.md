## Local research-asset lock

[`research-asset-lock.json`](research-asset-lock.json) pins the original research revision, the
prepared RB-Y1 source revision, and SHA-256 values for the Anubis and FFW_SG2 robot USDs, their
planning inputs, kitchen 813-00, and the prepared RB-Y1 URDF/USD dependency layers. The lock is
consumed by [`stage_research_assets.py`](../scripts/tools/stage_research_assets.py) and recorded
in the external bundle manifest. Staging creates ignored local links. The project maintainer has
authorized publication of project-controlled assets; this does not replace third-party asset
terms. The prepared RB-Y1 root USD is copied and normalized to a relative `model.usd` reference;
its derived hash is locked separately. Its source is supplied in the external bundle.
